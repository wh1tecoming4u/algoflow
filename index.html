How does the whale tracking work? ↓
We monitor all Polymarket on-chain transactions in real time. Any wallet moving $10,000+ is flagged and cross-referenced against historical win rates. When multiple high-performing wallets coordinate on the same market, PolyWhale fires a signal with full context and confidence scoring.
